# Privacy Policy for "Habit Tracker"

**Effective Date:** October 9, 2026

**Developer:** 4isper

## 1\. Overview

"Habit Tracker" (the "App") is an offline app for tracking daily habits: you create
habits, mark them done, and review your history, streaks, and completion rate.
This Privacy Policy explains what data is processed when you use the App and how
it is protected. We follow a minimal-data approach: the App requires no account,
has no backend, and never transmits your data anywhere.

## 2\. Data Stored on Your Device

The App processes only the information you enter yourself — a habit name, an
optional emoji and color, the habit's creation date, a day-by-day history of the
days you marked it as done, and an optional reminder time — solely to provide
the tracking experience.

- All data is stored **strictly locally** in the App's private storage: the app
  sandbox on iOS, internal app storage on Android. We do not transmit, upload,
  or store this data on any external server under our control.
- The App is built without network access. Android release builds are shipped
  without the `INTERNET` permission, and the App's privacy manifest declares
  that no data is collected and no tracking is performed.
- The App does not request or store personal information such as your name,
  email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, or advertising
  identifiers.
- The App reads a few system settings — your device language, your 24-hour clock
  preference, and your light or dark appearance — to format the interface. These
  values are used locally and are never stored or transmitted.

## 3\. Local Notifications

If you enable a reminder for a habit, the App schedules a **local** notification
at the time you chose. Reminders are scheduled entirely on your device: there is
no push server, no messaging service, and no device token is ever created or
sent.

- Permission is requested only when you turn a reminder on, never at first
  launch. If you decline, no reminder is scheduled.
- The notification displays the habit name you entered. On a locked device that
  text is visible on the lock screen, so if a habit name is personal to you,
  consider whether to enable reminders for it.

## 4\. Data Protection and Your Rights

- **Local data.** Because your habits are stored only on your device, you retain
  full control over them. You can delete any single habit — including its entire
  completion history — at any time from that habit's detail screen. You can erase
  all App data at any time by clearing the App's storage in your device settings
  or by uninstalling the App entirely.
- **System backups.** On Android, the App disables system backups, so its data
  is not included in your Google account backup. On iOS, App data may be
  included in your device's iCloud or Finder backup. This is handled by the
  operating system and is outside our control.
- **EEA users.** If you are in the European Economic Area, you have the right
  to access, rectify, or erase your personal data and to object to or restrict
  its processing, as well as to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.
  Because the App holds no personal data on any server and creates no account,
  these rights are exercised through the controls above. If you need anything
  beyond them, contact us and we will assist.

## 5\. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for a general audience and is not specifically directed at
children. It collects no personal information from anyone, children included. If
you believe information about a child has been collected, please contact us and
we will remove it.
